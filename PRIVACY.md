# Foldivo — Privacy Policy

Foldivo is published under the name **Otter Appworks**. Its legal operator is **Alexandre Wyer**, a sole proprietor in Switzerland, who is also the controller for information voluntarily provided to support. This policy applies to both the direct and Mac App Store editions. The app has no user account, advertisements, analytics, tracking,
developer-operated server or automatic crash-report upload. It does not collect
or transmit your files, filenames, folder permissions, usage or device identifiers
to the developer.

## Files and permissions

The app reads and changes files only as directed by you, within the access allowed by macOS. The App Store edition is limited to folders you grant
through macOS. Local security-scoped bookmarks remember your folder permissions.
Favorites, workspaces, preferences, commands for pending operations and shelf paths
are stored locally on your Mac (inside the app container for the App Store edition). These settings are not sent to us.
In the App Store edition, you can forget saved permissions in Settings → Folder access. Quit and reopen the
app to end active grants; active tasks retain access until the process exits.

File dates and sizes are used locally to display, sort and compare files. Temporary
archive previews stay in the app’s temporary storage and old previews are cleaned
after seven days when the app runs. Removing a file, extracting an archive or
editing a document may change files on your Mac or in a location you selected.

## System services and destinations you choose

Quick Look, opening files in other applications, Terminal, Finder, cloud folders,
network shares and App Store purchases are handled by macOS and the respective
application or provider. Copying to a chosen cloud/network destination can send
files to that provider at your instruction. We do not receive those files or your
network credentials. Apple handles purchase/payment information under its own
privacy policy; the app does not receive your payment card details.

## Support and websites

Support and feedback buttons open GitHub in your browser. GitHub has its own
privacy policy. If you voluntarily open a public issue, the information you post
is public and is processed by GitHub. Do not post private filenames, file contents,
passwords, access tokens or payment details. You may also choose to email the support address below. Email delivery is processed by the relevant mail providers; do not send credentials, payment details or private file contents. We use information you choose to
provide only to address the request. The app does not automatically attach logs. Copy support information creates a local, user-reviewed summary of app/system versions, architecture, UI language and region; it does not send the summary.

The direct edition can optionally share folder favorites with Finder on your Mac. Its default mode keeps favorites independent. These settings are not uploaded by Foldivo.

For questions or privacy requests, email [foldivo@elzuzu.ch.eu.org](mailto:foldivo@elzuzu.ch.eu.org) or see the project’s
[support page](https://github.com/elzuzu/foldivo-feedback/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).

## App Store privacy answers

The current edition does not collect data for the developer and does not track
users. The App Store privacy questionnaire should be answered **Data Not Collected**.
The account holder must recheck these answers if functionality or SDKs change.
